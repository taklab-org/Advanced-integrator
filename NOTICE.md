# Attribution and distribution scope

This package contains the authors' Ohta–Kawasaki integrator/proof source and
its original numerical dependencies. Existing file-level author credits,
copyright statements and license notices are retained. The source provenance
manifest records hashes of the files selected from the validated run trees;
portable entry points and documented orchestration changes are new additions.

INTLAB and MATLAB are external dependencies and are not redistributed.
Please cite S. M. Rump, *INTLAB — INTerval LABoratory*, in T. Csendes (ed.),
Developments in Reliable Computing, Kluwer, 1999, pp. 77–104, and follow the
license terms of the installed INTLAB version.

The authors' original project code and packaging are distributed under the
MIT license in LICENSE, as authorized by the copyright holder. This grant
does not replace third-party licenses.

The three copies of `defect/Chebyshev/standardChop.m` identify themselves as
copies/adaptations of Chebfun's routine by Jared Aurentz and Nick Trefethen.
They remain subject to Chebfun's BSD license, retained in
`third_party/CHEBFUN-LICENSE.txt`. The upstream license source is
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chebfun/chebfun/master/LICENSE.txt .

The local Git repository is prepared for review. Creating it does not upload
anything to GitHub, create a remote repository, or grant additional rights.
